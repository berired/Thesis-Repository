# Rabies vaccine

The rabies vaccine protects dogs and cats from rabies, a deadly disease that can also infect people.

## Why it matters
- WSAVA says that where rabies is endemic, vaccinating both dogs and cats should be considered essential, even if there is no legal requirement.
- In the Philippines, the Anti-Rabies Act (RA 9482, Sec. 5) requires owners to have their dogs regularly vaccinated against rabies, to keep a registration card recording the vaccinations, and to register their dogs. Dogs must not roam public places without a leash.
- A dog bite must be reported to the officials within 24 hours, and the dog placed under observation by a vet.
- Keep the vaccination card safe and bring it to vet visits. Keep the health record in this app up to date as well.

## Schedule
- Your vet decides the schedule. WSAVA advises against giving core vaccines more often than necessary in adult animals.

## After the vaccine
- Contact your vet if you notice anything unusual after a vaccination. WSAVA encourages vets to report possible adverse events.
