# Cephalexin

Cephalexin is an antibiotic. The Rilexine chewable tablet label is for dogs with a skin infection (superficial bacterial pyoderma). Prescription only.

## How to give it
- The tablets are made to taste good. Dogs can eat more than the dose if they can reach them, so store them out of reach of every pet, in a secured place.
- Food lowers the peak level of the drug in the blood but not the total amount absorbed, so it can be given with or without food.
- Give the course your vet set. The label course for skin infection is 28 days, so finish it unless your vet tells you to stop.

## Watch for
- The most common reactions in dogs are diarrhea, vomiting, loss of appetite and tiredness. Tell your vet if they continue or are severe.
- Contact your vet right away if your dog ate more tablets than prescribed, or if another pet ate any.
- Do not use in a dog with a known allergy to cephalexin, penicillins or cephalosporins.
- The label says safe use in breeding, pregnant or nursing dogs has not been evaluated, so tell your vet if this applies.

## Storage
- Store at 20 to 25 °C (68 to 77 °F). Keep out of reach of children. If a person swallows it by accident, call a doctor immediately.
