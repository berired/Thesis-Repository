# Clindamycin

Clindamycin is an antibiotic used in dogs and cats. Prescription only.

## How to give it
- Give it on the schedule your vet set (the label is every 12 hours). Treatment can run up to 28 days when the vet judges it is needed.
- Do not stop early because your pet looks better unless your vet says so.

## Watch for
- Vomiting and diarrhea are occasionally seen. The label says clindamycin can let other bacteria and yeast overgrow, so tell your vet if diarrhea is severe or does not stop.
- Do not use it in an animal that has had a reaction to clindamycin or lincomycin.
- Longer courses (a month or more) need periodic blood tests, so keep the check-ups your vet sets.

## Other pets
- It must not be given to rabbits, hamsters, guinea pigs, horses, chinchillas or ruminant animals because of serious stomach and bowel effects. If you have these animals at home, keep the medicine away from them.

## Storage
- Store at 20 to 25 °C (68 to 77 °F).
