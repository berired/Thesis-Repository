# Selamectin

Selamectin is a monthly liquid applied to the skin. It prevents heartworm and kills fleas and ear mites, and treats other parasites depending on species. Prescription only.

## How to apply it
- Use the whole tube, in the size your vet chose for your pet's weight. Apply it to the skin as shown on the package. Do not massage it into the skin.
- Do not apply it to broken skin or when the coat is wet. Bathing or shampooing 2 or more hours after treatment does not reduce its effect.
- Avoid contact between the product and your fingers. Keep children away from the treated area.
- The label recommends it for dogs 6 weeks and older and cats 8 weeks and older.

## Watch for
- A small bald or red patch where it was applied happened in about 1% of treated cats. Other reported signs include vomiting, loose stool or diarrhea, loss of appetite, tiredness, drooling, itching, tremors and seizures. Contact your vet if you see any of these.

## Safety for people
- The product contains alcohol. It can irritate human skin and eyes, and hives, itching or redness have been reported in people. Wash your hands after applying it, and keep it out of reach of children.
