# Core dog vaccine (distemper, adenovirus, parvovirus)

WSAVA's core vaccines for dogs protect against canine distemper virus, canine adenovirus (type 1) and canine parvovirus (type 2). These are often given together in one combination vaccine (such as DHPP). WSAVA says all dogs should receive them, after considering lifestyle and where they live or travel.

## Puppies
- Maternal antibodies from the mother can block early vaccines, so puppies need several doses 2 to 4 weeks apart, with the final dose at 16 weeks of age or older.
- WSAVA advises a booster at or after 26 weeks of age (not waiting until 12 to 16 months).
- A puppy that can only have one vaccination should have the core vaccines at 16 weeks or older.

## Adults
- WSAVA says core vaccines should not be given more often than necessary in adults: most modern core vaccines give protection that lasts many years.
- Your vet may offer a blood test from 20 weeks of age to check protection. Your vet decides the schedule.

## Other vaccines
- Respiratory vaccines are non-core for pet dogs in typical homes. Leptospirosis is highly recommended where it is endemic. Your vet will say which apply to your dog.

## After the vaccine
- Contact your vet if you notice anything unusual after a vaccination.
