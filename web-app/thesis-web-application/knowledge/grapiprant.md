# Grapiprant

Grapiprant is a pain and inflammation medicine for dogs with arthritis. It is a newer type of NSAID. Prescription only.

## How to give it
- Give it exactly as your vet told you. The label says to use the lowest effective dose for the shortest time that works.
- The label says taking the tablet with food lowers how much of the drug gets into the body. Ask your vet whether to give it on an empty stomach.
- Do not give it with other NSAIDs or steroids.

## Watch for: stop the medicine and call your vet immediately if you see
- Diarrhea (watery or bloody), vomiting or decreased appetite.
- Dark or tarry stools.
- Drinking or urinating more than usual.
- Pale gums, or yellow gums, skin or eyes.
- Tiredness, poor coordination or seizures.
- Your vet may check blood tests from time to time.

## Do not use if
- Your dog is allergic to grapiprant.
- The label says safety has not been evaluated in dogs under 9 months or under 8 lb (3.6 kg), or in breeding, pregnant or nursing dogs.
