# Enrofloxacin

Enrofloxacin is a fluoroquinolone antibiotic for bacterial infections in dogs and cats. Prescription only.

## How to give it
- The label says to continue for at least 2 to 3 days after the signs have gone. Severe or complicated infections can need up to 30 days. Follow the course your vet set and do not stop early.
- If there is no improvement within 5 days, the label says the vet should reassess, so tell your vet.

## Watch for
- Vomiting was uncommon in field studies and went away on its own (2 of 270 dogs). Tell your vet if it continues.
- Cats: rare cases of eye damage (retinal toxicity) have been linked to this drug, especially above the labeled dose. Give a cat exactly the prescribed amount and tell your vet at once if you notice any change in your cat's vision or eyes.
- It must not be used in an animal that is allergic to quinolone antibiotics.

## Growing dogs
- The label says it should not be used in small and medium breed dogs during rapid growth (about 2 to 8 months of age). Safety is not established in large breeds, which may still be growing up to a year, or giant breeds up to 18 months. Tell your vet your dog's age and size if you are not sure it was considered.
- Safety in breeding or pregnant cats has not been established.

## Storage
- Keep out of reach of children.
