# Afoxolaner flea and tick chewable

Afoxolaner is a monthly chewable that kills fleas and several kinds of ticks on dogs. Prescription only.

## How to give it
- Give it once a month. It can be given with or without food.
- Make sure your dog eats the whole dose and watch for a few minutes. If part of the dose was lost, or your dog vomited within 2 hours, the label says to give another full dose.

## Watch for
- The most commonly reported reaction is vomiting. Others include itching, tiredness, diarrhea, loss of appetite and allergic reactions (hives, swelling).
- This class of drug (isoxazolines) has been linked to nerve-related reactions: tremors, wobbliness and seizures, even in dogs with no history of seizures. Contact your vet immediately if you see any of these.
- If your dog has ever had seizures, tell your vet; the label says to use it with caution in dogs with a history of seizures or neurologic disease.
- The label says safety has not been evaluated in dogs under 8 weeks of age or under 4 lb.

## Safety
- Keep it out of reach of children, dogs, cats and other animals to prevent an overdose. Not for use in people.
