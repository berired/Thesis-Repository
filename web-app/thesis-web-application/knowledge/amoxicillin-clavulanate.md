# Amoxicillin-clavulanate

Amoxicillin-clavulanate is an antibiotic that combines a penicillin (amoxicillin) with clavulanate, which helps it work against more bacteria. Given as chewable tablets or drops in dogs and cats. Prescription only.

## How to give it
- Keep each tablet in its foil strip until the moment you use it. Even if a tablet breaks, your pet should eat all of the pieces.
- Follow the number of days your vet set. The label gives about 5 to 7 days, or until 48 hours after signs stop, for skin and soft-tissue infections, and 10 to 14 days or longer for urinary infections. Do not stop early because your pet looks better.
- If there is no response after a few days (5 days in dogs, 3 days in cats), the label says the case should be reassessed, so tell your vet.

## Watch for
- Reported effects include loss of appetite, tiredness, vomiting and diarrhea. Tell your vet if they continue or are severe.
- It can cause allergic reactions. Do not use it in an animal allergic to any penicillin or cephalosporin. Tell your vet about any known allergy.

## Safety and storage
- Store it where pets cannot reach it, because accidental ingestion can cause an overdose. Keep out of reach of children.
- People who are allergic to penicillins or cephalosporins can react to this drug, so take care when handling it.
- Store in a cool, dry place, not above 25 °C (77 °F).
