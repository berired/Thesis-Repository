# Meloxicam

Meloxicam is an anti-inflammatory pain medicine (an NSAID). The Metacam oral suspension label is for dogs with arthritis. Prescription only.

## How to give it
- Shake the bottle well. The liquid can be mixed with food or squirted directly into the mouth. Use the measuring syringe that comes with the bottle and measure carefully.
- Give it at the amount your vet set. If it is stopped or not given as directed, the pain may come back.
- Do not give it with aspirin, other NSAIDs (such as carprofen) or steroids (such as prednisone) unless your vet tells you to.

## Watch for: stop the medicine and call your vet immediately if you see
- Vomiting, diarrhea or decreased appetite.
- Dark or tarry stools.
- Drinking or urinating more than usual.
- Pale gums, or yellow gums, skin or eyes.
- Tiredness, poor coordination or behavior changes.
- Serious problems can happen without warning, and rarely can be fatal. Your vet may check blood tests from time to time.

## Cats
- **Do not give meloxicam to cats.** The label warns that repeated use in cats has been linked to sudden kidney failure and death. Call your vet if a cat takes it. A cat in your home must not get dog medicine.

## Do not use if
- Your dog is allergic to meloxicam, or had an allergic reaction (hives, facial swelling, red or itchy skin) to aspirin or another NSAID.
- The label says safety has not been evaluated in dogs under 6 months, breeding, pregnant or nursing dogs, and it is not recommended in dogs with bleeding disorders.
