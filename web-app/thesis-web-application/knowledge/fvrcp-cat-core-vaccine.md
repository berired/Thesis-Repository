# Core cat vaccine (panleukopenia, calicivirus, herpesvirus)

WSAVA's core vaccines for cats protect against feline parvovirus (panleukopenia), feline calicivirus and feline herpesvirus-1. These are usually given together in one combination vaccine (FVRCP). WSAVA says all cats should receive them.

## Kittens
- Maternal antibodies can block early vaccines, so kittens need several doses 2 to 4 weeks apart, with the final dose at 16 weeks of age or older.
- WSAVA advises a booster at or after 26 weeks of age.

## Adults
- WSAVA says core vaccines should not be given more often than necessary in adults: most modern core vaccines give protection that lasts many years. Your vet decides the schedule.

## Other vaccines
- Rabies vaccination is considered essential for cats where rabies is endemic. Feline leukemia (FeLV) vaccine is considered core for kittens under 1 year and for adult cats with outdoor access or that live with cats that go outdoors. Your vet will say which apply.

## After the vaccine
- Contact your vet if you notice anything unusual after a vaccination.
