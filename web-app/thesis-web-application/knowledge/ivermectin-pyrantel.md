# Ivermectin and pyrantel heartworm preventive

This monthly chewable prevents heartworm disease in dogs and also treats and controls roundworms and hookworms. Prescription only.

## How to give it
- Give it once a month, on or about the same day each month, during the period of the year when mosquitoes are active. The first dose must be given within a month of the dog's first mosquito exposure and the last within a month after the last exposure.
- If a dose is late, give it as soon as you remember and go back to the monthly schedule. The label says a gap of more than a month can reduce protection, so ask your vet if you are unsure.
- Encourage your dog to chew it and watch for a few minutes. Swallowing it whole carries a risk of choking or a blocked bowel. If part of the dose was lost or spat out, the label recommends giving another dose.
- When switching from another heartworm product, the first dose of this one must be given within a month of the last dose of the old one.

## Watch for
- Vomiting or diarrhea within 24 hours of a dose was seen in about 1% of doses. Other reports include tiredness, loss of appetite, drooling, itching, wobbliness, tremors and seizures. Contact your vet if you see any of these.
- Choking or blockage of the gut has been reported, so watch your dog as it eats the chew.
- The label recommends it for dogs 6 weeks of age and older, and says it is safe in pregnant and breeding dogs.

## Storage
- Store at 20 to 25 °C (68 to 77 °F), away from light. Keep it where dogs, cats and other pets cannot reach it, to prevent an overdose.
