# Fenbendazole

Fenbendazole is a dewormer for dogs. It treats roundworms, hookworms, whipworms and one kind of tapeworm (Taenia).

## How to give it
- Mix the daily dose into a small amount of your dog's usual food (moisten dry food if needed) and make sure your dog eats all of it.
- It is given once a day for 3 days in a row. Weigh your dog so you use the right amount.
- The label's general schedule: puppies at 6, 8, 10 and 12 weeks of age, with the mother treated at the same time, and adult dogs over 6 months at least twice a year. Your vet may set a different schedule.

## Watch for
- Vomiting was seen in about 1% of treated dogs. Tell your vet if it continues.
- It only kills Taenia tapeworms. If you still see tapeworm segments in the stool afterward, talk to your vet.
- Do not deworm a dog that is sick. Check with your vet first.
- The label says it is safe in puppies 6 weeks and older and in adult dogs, including pregnant dogs.

## Storage
- Store at room temperature, 20 to 25 °C (68 to 77 °F). Keep out of reach of children.
