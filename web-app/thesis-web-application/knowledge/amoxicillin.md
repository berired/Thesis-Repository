# Amoxicillin

Amoxicillin is a penicillin-type antibiotic for bacterial infections in dogs and cats. It is a prescription medicine.

## How to give it
- The label says it is stable in stomach acid and may be given without regard to meals, so it can be given with or without food.
- The label says treatment continues until at least 48 hours after all signs have gone, so do not stop early just because the pet looks better. Follow the course your vet set.
- The label tells the vet to reassess if there is no improvement after 5 days, so tell your vet if your pet is not getting better.

## Watch for
- Amoxicillin is a penicillin and can cause allergic reactions. Contact your vet right away if you notice signs of an allergic reaction.
- It must not be used in an animal that has had an allergic reaction to penicillin before. Tell your vet if your pet has any known allergy.

## Storage
- Keep the bottle tightly closed and do not store above 25 °C (77 °F). Keep it out of reach of children and other pets.
